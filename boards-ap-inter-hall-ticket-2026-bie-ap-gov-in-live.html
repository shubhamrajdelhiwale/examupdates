AP Inter Hall Tickets 2026 Released: Download Hall Tickets Now

By Sakshi Education

The Board of Intermediate Education Andhra Pradesh (BIEAP) has officially released the AP Intermediate Public Examination Hall Tickets 2026.

AP Inter Hall Ticket 2026 – Download 1st & 2nd Year Admit Card

Students of both 1st and 2nd year can download their hall tickets from the official website starting Friday at 11:00 AM.

Students can visit the official website https://bie.ap.gov.in to download their admit cards.

How to Download AP Inter Hall Ticket 2026

  • Second Year Students: Enter First Year Hall Ticket Number.
  • First Year Students: Enter SSC (10th Class) Hall Ticket Number or Aadhaar Number along with Date of Birth.
  • Hall tickets can also be accessed through the Mana Mithra WhatsApp service (9552300009) by sending a “Hi” message and following the instructions.
  • College managements have been instructed to download hall tickets through their respective logins and distribute them to students.

AP Inter Public Exam Dates 2026

  • First Year Exams: Begin from February 23, 2026
  • Second Year Exams: Begin from February 24, 2026
  • Exam Timings: 9:00 AM to 12:00 Noon
  • Last Date of Exams: March 24, 2026

Revised Exam Dates

  • Second Year Mathematics and Civics papers scheduled on March 3 will now be conducted on March 4.
  • First Year Public Administration and Logic papers scheduled on March 20 will now be held on March 21.

The changes were made due to public holidays:

  • March 3 – Holi
  • March 20 – Ramzan

All other exams will be conducted as per the previously announced schedule. Students are advised to download their hall tickets well in advance and carefully check all details mentioned on the admit card.

AP Inter Admit Cards 2026: Exam Day Guidelines

Candidates appearing for their AP Intermediate 1st and 2nd year exam 2026 must make sure they download their admit cards from the website. Candidates must also keep in mind the important guidelines for the exams.

  • AP Inter admit card is a mandatory document to be carried by students to the exam centre
  • Students need to make sure the college stamp/seal and principal’s signature is affixed before the exams commence
  • Candidates must reach the centre atleast an hour before the exams
  • Students need to carry with them the hard copy of their admit cards
  • Mobile phones, tablets, smart watches, bluetooth devices, calculators and other electronic devices are not permitted inside the exam hall
  • Students will be given time to read the question paper before attempting the exam
  • Students cannot leave the exam hall before the exams conclude
  • Candidate caught cheating cannot continue with the exams
